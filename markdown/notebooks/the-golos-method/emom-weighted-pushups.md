# EMOM Weighted Push-Ups

## Description

The 10-minute EMOM of push-ups is the pressing pillar of the Golos Method. Every minute on the minute, you drop and perform a set of push-ups with added load — typically a weight plate on your back, a weighted vest, or a sandbag. When the reps are complete, the remaining time in that minute is your rest. When the next minute begins, you go again.

## Whiteboard Script

### Beginner — Bodyweight Foundation
```time
(10) 1:00 EMOM
  5 Pushup
```

### Intermediate — Light Load
```time
(10) 1:00 EMOM
  3 Weighted Pushup 10|20|30lb
```

### Advanced — Heavy Load
```time
(10) 1:00 EMOM
  3 Weighted Pushup 45|65|85|105lb
```
